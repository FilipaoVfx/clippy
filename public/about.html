A clipboard that crosses devices

Pastty does one everyday job: getting text, a photo or a short video from the device in your hand to the one in front of you, without emailing it to yourself.

Open Pastty Free · No app · No account

What Pastty is

Pastty is a free online clipboard that runs in the browser. One device opens a room, the others join with a short code or a QR, and whatever you send appears on the rest straight away. It works on phones, tablets and computers, on any network, with nothing to install.

It is deliberately small: text up to 10 KB, images up to 5 MB, videos up to 50 MB, and up to 5 devices in a room.

How it is built

  • A relay, not a store

    Pastty runs on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects. Each room is a short-lived relay that passes items to the devices connected at that moment and keeps none of them.

  • Rooms that expire

    A room lasts 5 minutes from the last join or reconnection, then it is gone.

  • No accounts

    The code is the only thing tying your devices together. No email, no password, no profile.

  • Encrypted in transit

    Everything travels over HTTPS and secure WebSockets. It is not end-to-end encrypted, and the pages say so plainly.

How it stays free

Pastty is free and shows no ads. If ads are ever added to help with hosting, they will appear only on guide pages, never on the clipboard screen where you paste and receive.
