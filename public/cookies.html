Cookies and browser storage

Pastty keeps a few small things in your browser so the app works. Here is each one, what it is for and how to remove it.

Open Pastty Free · No app · No account

What Pastty saves on your device

ItemWhereWhat it is for
pastty_themeLocal storageRemembers the light or dark theme. Stays until you clear site data.
clippy_sessionLocal storageLets a reload put you back in your room: its code, your random device id, a reconnection token and when it expires. Removed when you leave the room or it expires.
App filesCache storagePastty’s own pages, styles and scripts, kept by its service worker so it opens fast and can be installed. Replaced on every update.

Cookies

Pastty sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics cookies.

Cloudflare, which delivers the site, may set a strictly necessary security cookie such as __cf_bm or cf_clearance when it needs to tell people from bots. Cloudflare explains its cookies.

No advertising

No page on Pastty shows ads or loads advertising cookies.

How to clear it

Leaving a room removes the session. To remove everything, clear this site’s data in your browser settings, usually under Privacy or Site settings. Pastty keeps working; it only forgets your theme.
