Copy text from your phone to your computer

Stop emailing yourself. Open Pastty on both screens, paste on the phone, and the text is waiting on the computer, ready to copy.

Open Pastty Free · No app · No account

Send text from phone to PC in three steps

  1. 1

    Open Pastty on the computer

    Go to pastty.com and press Create room. A code and a QR appear.

  2. 2

    Scan the QR with your phone

    Point the camera at the QR and open the link. The phone joins the room without typing anything.

  3. 3

    Paste and send

    On the phone, paste the text into the box and press Send. On the computer, press Copy on the item and paste it wherever you need it.

The small things it is good for

  • Links

    A page you found on the phone, opened on the big screen without retyping the address.

  • Codes and numbers

    Tracking numbers, booking references, Wi-Fi passwords, a one-time code you need on the laptop.

  • Notes and drafts

    A paragraph you wrote on the bus, moved into the document on your desk.

  • Both directions

    The computer can send to the phone just the same: an address for the maps app, a message to finish later.

Works across iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and Linux

Pastty runs in any modern browser: Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Samsung Internet. There is no app to install on either side, so it works on a work laptop where you cannot install software, on a borrowed computer, or on a Linux desktop that phone makers forgot about.

You can also add Pastty to your home screen. It installs as a small web app and opens straight to the pairing screen.

Copying text from phone to computer: questions

Does Pastty copy my phone clipboard automatically?

No. Browsers do not let a web page read your clipboard in the background, and Pastty would not want to. You paste the text and press Send.

How much text can I send at once?

Up to 10 KB per message, which is several pages of plain text. For longer documents, send them in parts.

Is the text saved anywhere?

No. It is relayed to the devices in your room at that moment and is not stored on the server. It stays on screen until you close the room or reload.
