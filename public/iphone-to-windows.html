Copy and paste between an iPhone and a Windows PC

Apple’s Universal Clipboard stops at the Mac. Pastty works in Safari on the iPhone and in any browser on Windows, so the two can finally share a clipboard.

Open Pastty Free · No app · No account

Set it up once, in under a minute

  1. 1

    On the Windows PC

    Open pastty.com in Edge, Chrome or Firefox and press Create room.

  2. 2

    On the iPhone

    Open the Camera, point it at the QR on your screen and tap the link. Safari opens Pastty already in the room.

  3. 3

    Copy across

    Paste on one device, press Send, and copy it on the other. It works both ways.

Make it feel like a native feature

On the iPhone, tap Share › Add to Home Screen in Safari. Pastty gets an icon and opens full screen. On Windows, Edge and Chrome can install it as an app from the address bar, so it lives in the Start menu next to everything else.

Because it is all browser-based, it also works between an iPad and a Windows laptop, or between an iPhone and a Chromebook.

iPhone and Windows: questions

Does this replace Phone Link?

For moving text and pictures, often yes. Phone Link does much more, but its iPhone support is limited. Pastty only needs a browser on both sides.

Do I need iCloud or a Microsoft account?

No account of any kind. The pairing code is the only thing that links the two devices.

Does the iPhone need to stay on Pastty?

Keep the tab open while you send. If the phone locks, Pastty reconnects when you come back, as long as it returns within a minute.
