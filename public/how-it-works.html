Two devices, one code, a few seconds

Pastty is a shared clipboard that lives in your browser. One device opens a room, the other joins with a code, and anything you send lands on the other side straight away.

Open Pastty Free · No app · No account

From zero to connected

  1. 1

    Open pastty.com and create a room

    Press Create room. Your device gets a short code like KDR-48M and a QR code. Nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

  2. 2

    Join from the other device

    Scan the QR with the phone camera, or open pastty.com and type the code under Join a session. A room lasts 5 minutes, and every join or reconnection renews the timer.

  3. 3

    Send

    Paste text and press Send, or drop in a photo or a video. It shows up under Received on every other device in the room, with a button to copy or download it.

What happens behind the screen

  • A live connection

    Each device keeps an encrypted WebSocket (WSS) open to Pastty. When you send, the relay passes the item to the devices connected right then.

  • Nothing kept

    Items are relayed, not stored: there is no history on the server and no inbox to come back to. Close the room and it is gone.

  • Room for five

    Up to 5 devices can share one room: your phone, tablet, laptop and desktop at once.

  • Drops are fine

    If a device loses its connection it rejoins on its own. It has 60 seconds to come back before it leaves the room.

Good to know

  • Both devices need to be online at the same time. Pastty is a live link, not a place to leave files for later.
  • Pastty does not read your clipboard on its own. You decide what to paste and send.
  • Anyone with your code can join while it is valid, so only share it with people you trust.
  • Sizes: text up to 10 KB per message, images up to 5 MB, videos up to 50 MB. See all limits.

Questions about pairing

Do both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

No. Pairing goes through the code, so a phone on mobile data and a laptop on office Wi-Fi can share a room.

Can I pair more than two devices?

Yes. Up to 5 devices can join the same room. Everything you send reaches all the others.

What if the room expires?

Create a new one. A new code takes a second, and joining with it renews the timer for everyone.
