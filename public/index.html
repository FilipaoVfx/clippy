Your shared text, images and videos will land here.

Two devices. One little room.

  1. Create a room. Your first device gets a short code and a QR.
  2. Make the connection. Open this same address on your other device and scan or enter the code.
  3. Send it across. Share text, images up to 5 MB, or videos up to 50 MB with up to five devices.

Rooms last five minutes; joining or reconnecting renews the timer. Content is relayed over your connection; it isn’t saved as message history. Anyone with your code can join, so share it with people you trust.

Less sending to yourself. More getting on with it.
