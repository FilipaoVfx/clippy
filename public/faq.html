The basics

What is Pastty?

A shared clipboard in the browser. Pair your phone, tablet and computers with a short code, then send text, photos and videos between them in real time.

Is Pastty free?

Yes. There is no paid plan and no account.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Pastty runs in any modern browser. You can add it to your home screen or install it as an app if you like.

Which devices work?

Anything with a current browser: iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS.

Pairing

How do I connect two devices?

Press Create room on one. On the other, scan the QR or type the code under Join a session. See how it works.

How long does a room last?

5 minutes, and every join or reconnection renews the timer. After that, create a new room.

How many devices can join?

Up to 5 in one room.

Do the devices need the same Wi-Fi?

No. They can be on different networks, or on mobile data.

Sending

What can I send?

Text and links (up to 10 KB per message), images in PNG, JPG or WEBP (up to 5 MB) and videos in MP4, WEBM or MOV (up to 50 MB). See limits.

Does Pastty read my clipboard automatically?

No. You paste what you want to share and press Send.

Why did my item not arrive?

Pastty only delivers to devices connected at that moment. If the other device closed the tab or lost its connection for more than a minute, join again and resend.

Privacy and safety

Is my content stored?

No. Items are relayed to the devices in your room and not kept on the server. There is no history to come back to.

Is it encrypted?

Everything travels over HTTPS and secure WebSockets (WSS), so it is encrypted in transit. It is not end-to-end encrypted: the relay handles the item while passing it on.

Who can join my room?

Anyone who has the code while it is valid. Share it only with people you trust, and leave the room when you are done.
