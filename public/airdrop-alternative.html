An AirDrop alternative that works on any device

AirDrop is great until someone in the room has Windows or Android. Pastty does the everyday part (a link, a note, a photo, a short video) on anything with a browser.

When Pastty is the better pick

  • Mixed devices

    An iPhone and a Windows laptop, or an Android phone and a Mac.

  • Different networks

    Your phone on mobile data, the computer on office Wi-Fi.

  • Locked-down computers

    Work machines where you cannot install software still have a browser.

AirDrop alternative: questions

Is Pastty peer-to-peer like AirDrop?

No. Items go through Pastty’s relay over an encrypted connection and are passed on without being stored. That is why it works across networks and distances.

Can it send large files?

Videos up to 50 MB and images up to 5 MB. For bigger files or other file types, a dedicated file-transfer service is a better fit.
