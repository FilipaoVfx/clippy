Copy and paste between Android and your PC

No companion app, no USB debugging, no account to link. Open Pastty in Chrome on the phone and in any browser on the computer.

Open Pastty Free · No app · No account

Android to computer, step by step

  1. 1

    Computer: create a room

    Open pastty.com and press Create room.

  2. 2

    Phone: scan the QR

    Use the camera or Google Lens on the QR, or type the code on pastty.com.

  3. 3

    Send either way

    Paste text or pick a photo and press Send. Copy or download it on the other side.

Works with the computer you have

  • Windows

    Edge, Chrome or Firefox. Install it from the address bar to keep it one click away.

  • Mac

    Safari or Chrome, for when your phone is not an iPhone.

  • Linux and ChromeOS

    Any current browser. No packages, no drivers.

Android and PC: questions

Do I need Quick Share or Nearby Share?

No. Those need compatible devices close to each other. Pastty works through the browser over any network.

Can I add Pastty to the Android home screen?

Yes. In Chrome, open the menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

Does it work on mobile data?

Yes. The phone and the computer do not need to share a network.
