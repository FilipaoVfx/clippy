Send photos and videos from your phone to your computer

No cable, no cloud folder, no chat app squeezing your pictures. Pair the phone with the computer and drop the photo in.

Open Pastty Free · No app · No account

How to send a photo to your PC

  1. 1

    Create a room on the computer

    Open pastty.com and press Create room.

  2. 2

    Join with the phone

    Scan the QR with the camera, or type the code on pastty.com.

  3. 3

    Pick the photo or video

    Under Send Image or Send Video, press Browse file and choose it. On the computer, press Download.

What you can send

TypeFormatsUp to
ImagesPNG, JPG, WEBP5 MB
VideosMP4, WEBM, MOV50 MB
TextPlain text, links10 KB per message

Images travel as the original file, so they arrive at full resolution. Long videos move in chunks with a progress bar on both sides.

Why not just use a cable or the cloud?

  • No cable hunting

    Works over the internet, so the phone can stay in your pocket on mobile data.

  • No cloud copy

    The photo goes from one device to the other and is not kept on a server.

  • No compression

    Chat apps shrink pictures. Pastty sends the file you picked.

Sending photos: questions

Can I send iPhone HEIC photos?

Pastty accepts PNG, JPG and WEBP. When you choose a photo from the iPhone photo picker in Safari, iOS usually hands it over as a JPG. If a photo is refused, set Settings › Camera › Formats to Most Compatible for new pictures.

Can I send several photos at once?

One file at a time. Send them one after the other; each lands on the other device as soon as it arrives.

Can I send documents like PDFs?

Not yet. Pastty handles text, images and videos. For a document, share a link to it as text.
