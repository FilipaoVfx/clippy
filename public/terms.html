Terms of use

By using Pastty you agree to these terms. They are short and meant to be read.

Open Pastty Free · No app · No account

The service

Pastty is a free service in the browser that relays text, images and videos between devices that join the same room with a short code. Items reach the devices connected at that moment and are not stored. A room expires 5 minutes after the last join or reconnection.

Pastty may change, pause or stop at any time, and its limits may change with it.

What you may not do

  • Send anything that is illegal where you or the people in your room are.
  • Send content that sexualises, exploits or endangers minors.
  • Send malware or anything meant to harm devices or data.
  • Share content you have no right to share, or that infringes someone else’s rights.
  • Harass, threaten or spam people.
  • Join rooms you were not invited to, for example by guessing codes.
  • Overload or probe the service, work around its limits, or use automated tools to do so.

Your content

What you send stays yours, and you are responsible for it. You give Pastty only the permission it needs to pass each item to the devices in your room. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Room codes

Anyone with a valid code can join the room and see what is sent from then on. Give codes only to people you trust and close the room when you are done. Pastty cannot tell who is behind a device.
