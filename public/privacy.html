What Pastty does with your data

The short version: no accounts, nothing you send is stored, and rooms disappear on their own.

Open Pastty Free · No app · No account

In plain words

  • No account

    Pastty never asks for your name, email or phone number.

  • Content is not stored

    Text, images and videos are relayed to the devices in your room at that moment and are not saved on the server.

  • Short-lived rooms

    A room lasts 5 minutes from the last join or reconnection, then it is deleted with everything the relay knew about it.

  • Encrypted in transit

    All traffic uses HTTPS and secure WebSockets. Pastty is not end-to-end encrypted.

What the service does keep, briefly

To run a room, the relay keeps its code, random device identifiers and a reconnection token for each device, and the time they were last seen. These are deleted when the room expires.

Your IP address is attached to your open connection to limit abuse (how much one address can send per minute). It is dropped when the connection closes and is never stored with your content.

Your theme choice and your current session are saved in your own browser’s storage, so a reload can put you back in the room. You can clear them by leaving the room or clearing site data.

Who runs the infrastructure

Pastty runs on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects. Cloudflare processes each connection to deliver the service and keeps operational logs under its own privacy policy. Pastty itself adds no analytics, advertising or third-party trackers.

Keep your code to yourself

Anyone with a valid code can join your room and see what you send next. Share codes only with people you trust, and press the close button when you are done.
