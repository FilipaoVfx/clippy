Contact

One address for everything: hello@pastty.com. A message with the details below gets a faster answer.

Open Pastty Free · No app · No account

What to include

  • A bug

    The device and browser on each side, what you did and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

  • Misuse

    What happened and roughly when. Codes expire and nothing is stored, so there is no content to look up later: describe what you saw.

  • Privacy

    Questions about what the service handles. The privacy page answers most of them.

  • Anything else

    Ideas, press, partnerships or advertising.

Before you write

Pastty cannot recover anything you sent: items are relayed to the devices in your room and never stored. If a room closed, open a new one.

Many problems already have an answer in the FAQ or on the limits page.
